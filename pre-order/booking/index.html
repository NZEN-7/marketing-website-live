What happens after you book

  1. You pay the $990 booking deposit: that is all that is needed to hold a place in the build schedule.
  2. We assess your home from your gas bills, your heating layout, and a site visit or photos.
  3. We send you a written proposal with your system, your price and expected timing. Nothing more to pay until you accept it.
  4. You accept, and your deposit comes off the price. $3,000 is due when we confirm your system is in a production order, and the balance on commissioning. Changed your mind before then? The deposit is refunded in full.
  5. Delivery and commissioning: your licensed installer does the plumbing and electrical, and we commission and monitor the system remotely.
