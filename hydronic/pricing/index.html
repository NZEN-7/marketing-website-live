Already Got a Quote? Here's How to Compare It.

Most people who talk to us are holding a quote from someone else. That's a good position to be in, but the quotes are rarely comparing the same thing. Before you weigh price against price, check what each one actually covers:

  • Does it include storage? A heat pump without thermal storage heats on demand, which means it draws power at the exact hours power costs the most. Same comfort, very different running cost.
  • Does it include hot water? Often quoted separately, or left on your existing gas unit, which keeps your gas connection and its daily supply charge alive.
  • Does it include ductwork or new emitters? Worth asking directly. Ducted quotes in particular can carry significant work that isn't obvious in the headline number.
  • Is the price fixed? Ask whether switchboard work, pipework changes and access are inside the number or flagged as variations later.

We're happy to look at a quote you've been given and tell you what it does and doesn't cover, even if you go with it. Send it through with your quote request, or bring it to the assessment.
