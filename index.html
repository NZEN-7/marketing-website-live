Stop Wasting Your Solar Energy

Your panels make most of their power between 9am and 3pm. But your home needs energy most at dawn and dusk, when the sun's gone.

So you export cheap solar by day, then buy expensive grid power at peak. With feed-in tariffs near zero and "sun taxes" coming, that exported solar has gone from a credit to a cost.

Your battery wasn't built for heating

You've probably already noticed it. Run the air conditioner for an hour and watch the battery drop off a cliff. Heating and cooling are the biggest loads in your home by a long way, and a home battery is sized for the lights, the fridge and the telly.

Now picture electrifying a 30kW gas boiler. Even through an efficient heat pump, that's more power than most home batteries can deliver, let alone sustain for an evening. You'd flatten it before dinner was over, with nothing left for the rest of the house.

Thermal Dawn stores the energy as heat instead, in a store built for that one job and nothing else. Across our first two homes, the heat pump stayed off through the entire 5pm to 9pm peak on 59 of the last 63 evenings. No lithium, and it isn't competing with the rest of your house for what's left in the tank.

Fossil Fuels Keep Getting More Expensive

Every time you heat or cool at dawn or dusk, you're paying premium rates for fossil-fuel power. Thermal Dawn shifts that demand to your own solar hours, cutting bills and emissions while you stay comfortable year-round.

How it runs

It charges when power is cheapest, whether that's a free window, your own solar, or late-night off-peak. Then it holds the heat and runs your house through the evening with the compressor off.

Radiators or a concrete slab, it's the same hardware and the same software. You set your tariff and the temperature you want. It handles the rest.

About us

At Thermal Dawn, we're building the infrastructure Australia needs to shift from imported lithium batteries to sovereign thermal systems that balance our grid, starting with your home. Our thermal batteries solve the problem imported lithium can't: capturing abundant dawn solar and storing it as heating, cooling, and hot water for the peak-demand hours when grid power costs the most.

Our technology is developed in Australia, and our thermal stores are built and assembled at our own production facility in Hornsby, New South Wales, from Australian materials. The store is built to last decades, not the few years before a lithium battery degrades. Thermal Dawn is supported by Cicada Innovations, EnergyLab, and the NSW Office of the Chief Scientist and Engineer, backed by an Investment NSW grant, and a member of the Startmate accelerator. The heat pump is a proven unit we source rather than build; the storage, the controls, and the intelligence that runs them are ours. It's the thermal infrastructure Australia needs, engineered by Australians who live here.
